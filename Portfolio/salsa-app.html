Salsa App

Overview

The Salsa App Prototype is a mobile-first application designed to help dancers practice salsa step combinations and routines on the go. It aims to replicate a guided class experience with interactive features such as voice cues, tempo control, and a visual gallery of moves—making at-home practice more intuitive and structured.

‍

Goals

  1. Provide guided salsa step practice with audio and visual cues.
  2. Enable users to build and review combinations at their own pace.
  3. Offer practice modes for both solo and partner work.
  4. Maintain simple yet expressive UI that feels lively and easy to use.

‍

Target Audience

  • Beginners aiming to internalize salsa basics.
  • Intermediate dancers developing muscle memory through repetition.
  • Instructors and schools looking for supplemental training tools.

Key Features

  1. Step Library: Browse, select, and preview individual salsa moves with descriptions or footstep animation.
  2. Combo Builder: Add moves to custom routines; save and replay in sequence.
  3. Audio Guide: Voice cues announce next step at the beat. Audio cues with instruments for musicality and more advanced learners.
  4. Visual Assist: Optional icons or footsteps to reinforce timing visually.
  5. Tempo Control: Adjustable BPM to practice slower or faster depending on skill.
  6. Mode Selection: Filter between solo practice and partner routines.

‍

Design Process

Research & User Insights

  • Six years of personal experience in salsa dancing.
  • Interviews with salsa dancers and instructors to identify specific salsa moves, footwork, musicality, rhythm, tempo, pace, partner work, and solo work.
  • Competitive analysis of existing dance apps focused on rhythm, voice guidance, or combo libraries.

User Flow & Wireframing

  • Mapping key paths: Browse step library → Play combination → Practice mode → Tempo controls.
  • Creating low-fidelity sketches and wireframes to solidify screen hierarchy and navigation between modes.

Visual Design

  • Vibrant color palette with Latin flair—warm reds, energetic oranges, and deep tones for clarity at night or in dim dance studios.
  • Bold typography and iconography inspired by dance motion and rhythm.
  • Rhythm-themed UI elements like waveform indicators, counters, or animated footstep markers.

Prototyping

  • High-fidelity screens modeled in Figma or Adobe XD.
  • Key screens include: Home, Step Library, Combo Mode, Solo Practice, Tempo Settings, Audio Guide.

Challenges

  • Explaining steps clearly: Added descriptive labels and optional visual guides.
  • Tempo flexibility: Introduced BPM slider and preset speeds
  • User customization: Combo Builder feature supports saved routines.

‍

Final Thoughts

This Salsa App Prototype elegantly puts a digital salsa buddy in your pocket. If you need help learning steps, or if you forget moves, or if you want to get creative and make dance combos, this app will help you in your salsa dancing endeavors. Its playful yet structured UI makes repetition engaging without overwhelming, making it a strong concept for personal practice or dance studio integration.
