About Me

My name is Jess

I don’t just design websites—I design experiences that tell your story. Hi, I’m Jess—a web designer, developer, graphic artist, and filmmaker with a passion for visual storytelling and digital design. Whether I’m coding a sleek interface, designing a logo, or building out a full marketing campaign, I approach every project with curiosity, strategy, and creativity.

I’ve worn many hats over the years—working with Fortune 500 companies, small businesses, nonprofits, and solo entrepreneurs. My journey has taken me through the worlds of frontend development, branding, marketing, UX/UI design, video production, and beyond.

With a background in Film Studies, Studio Art, and Full Stack Development, I blend technical skills with artistic vision. The result? Digital experiences that are both functional and memorable.

Services

What I do.

Web Design & Development

I create responsive, user-friendly websites from concept to deployment. This includes wireframing, prototyping, frontend coding (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React), and integrating backend functionality using full-stack technologies. I ensure each site is visually compelling, SEO-optimized, and mobile-ready.

Includes: UI/UX design, responsive layouts, CMS integration, SEO best practices, accessibility compliance.

Multimedia & Graphic Design

I deliver high-impact visuals across digital and print mediums, including logos, advertisements, social media assets, infographics, brochures, and video production. You use Adobe Creative Suite, Sketch, Figma, and more to tell stories visually and support brand identity.

Includes: Branding, digital illustration, motion graphics, print design, photo/video editing, visual storytelling.

Marketing & Content Strategy Support

I blend artistic direction with strategic insight to create marketing assets that are both visually compelling and grounded in audience understanding. I use market and competitor research to guide design choices, ensuring that every creative output resonates with the target demographic and aligns with brand goals. My approach combines aesthetics with data, resulting in visuals that not only capture attention but also support meaningful engagement and campaign performance

Includes: Artistic concept development, audience analysis, competitor research, trend forecasting, brand-aligned visual systems, storytelling across marketing channels

Creative Storytelling & Narrative Design

I craft impactful narratives that elevate brands, products, and user experiences. With a background in film, digital art, and UX, I understand how to shape a story visually and emotionally—whether it's through a website's flow, a video campaign, or a brand's identity. My storytelling connects with audiences, drives engagement, and brings clarity to complex ideas.

Includes: Brand storytelling, UX narrative flow, scriptwriting, video scripting, campaign messaging, storyboarding, and content hierarchy.
