Yanis Marshall Workshop

Overview

This case study highlights an immersive dance workshop led by Yannis Marshall, the internationally renowned choreographer and performer celebrated for his groundbreaking work in high-heel choreography. Known for pushing artistic boundaries and empowering dancers to embrace confidence and individuality, Yannis brought his distinctive style to the workshop stage, blending technical mastery with fearless self-expression.

‍

Yannis Marshall

Yannis Marshall is a choreographer and dance performer from France who has built a global reputation for his unique dance style, stemming from classical ballet and jazz techniques. He first rose to international fame as a finalist on Britain’s Got Talent with his all-male high-heel dance trio. Since then, he has toured worldwide, teaching workshops, headlining performances, and collaborating with major artists including Céline Dion, Mariah Carey, and Beyoncé.

Marshall is celebrated not only for his technical brilliance but also for his commitment to inclusivity and empowerment through dance. His workshops challenge traditional gender norms in performance, inspiring dancers of all identities to embody confidence, individuality, and power.

My Skills Applied

My skills used for this project include:

  • Videography: To create a safe, inclusive space where dancers could explore self-expression and confidence through movement.
  • Video Editing: To teach people how to dance in high heels without falling.
  • Communication: To teach participants the technical precision and artistry of Yannis’ choreography in solo and group settings.
  • Camera Framing: To teach dancers Yannis’ style of dancing through ballet and jazz techniques.

‍

Goals

The workshop was designed to teach people how to dance like Yannis Marshall in heels successfully.

  1. Empowerment: To create a safe, inclusive space where dancers could explore self-expression and confidence through movement.
  2. Balance: To teach people how to dance in high heels without falling.
  3. Choreography: To teach participants the technical precision and artistry of Yannis’ choreography in solo and group settings.
  4. Style: To teach dancers Yannis’ style of dancing through ballet and jazz techniques.
  5. Performance Experience: To give dancers the opportunity to perform choreography in a professional, high-energy environment.

Workshop Experience

  • Warm-Up & Foundation: The session began with a technical warm-up that emphasized alignment, strength, and balance — crucial skills for executing choreography in heels.
  • Choreography Instruction: Yannis taught an original routine that highlighted his signature style: sharp yet fluid movements, dynamic use of musicality, and a balance of sensuality and strength.
  • Performance Coaching: Beyond movement, Yannis emphasized stage presence, emotional connection to the music, and the confidence needed to “own the room.”
  • Community Atmosphere: The workshop fostered a strong sense of support and camaraderie, with dancers encouraging one another while learning challenging material.

‍

Challenges

  • Dancing in Heels: Many participants faced the technical challenge of balancing technique with performance while wearing heels.
  • Pushing Beyond Comfort Zones: Yannis encouraged dancers to step outside of their usual movement styles, demanding both vulnerability and boldness.

‍

Achievements & Impact

Yannis Marshall’s workshop reinforced his role as a global leader in redefining performance and gender expression in dance. For participants, it was not only a technical masterclass but also a transformative experience that expanded their artistry, confidence, and sense of identity.

Britain's Got Talent (2014)

Yanis Marshall, along with two male dancers, gained significant recognition as a finalist on the eighth season of the show, known for his innovative heels choreography.

Dancing with the Stars

He served as an artistic coach on the French version of the popular dance competition show.

So You Think You Can Dance

He choreographed for the Ukrainian version of the show, showcasing his versatility and talent.

Cirque du Soleil

Yanis Marshall became the choreographer for the Las Vegas show Zumanity, continuing to expand his influence in large-scale productions.
